1. Why Does [formGroup]="control" Work Here?

  • Reason:

    Each item in your FormArray is explicitly created as a FormGroup (via this.fb.group() in buildForm()).

  • Key Point:

    [formGroup] expects a FormGroup, and your FormArray (this.form) contains only FormGroup items. The type assertion as FormGroup[] in the f() getter is safe because of this.

2. Why No formGroupName or formArrayName Directives?

  • Reason:

    You're binding each row directly to a FormGroup using [formGroup]="control". This is valid when:

    • You don't need a parent FormGroup wrapper (your <form> has no [formGroup] binding).
    • You're directly iterating over FormGroup items from the FormArray.
